OUR VISION

Why we do sushi this way

We started Min Sushi and Ramen Bowl with a simple idea: great sushi shouldn't only happen on special occasions. So we kept the care; the daily fish run, the hand-pressed rice, the chef who's been doing this for 10 years and dropped everything else. No stiff dining rooms. No menus you need translated.

Fresh meat daily

Our fish arrives every morning and we only prep what we'll serve that day. What doesn't sell doesn't come back.

Made when you order

Nothing sits under a light waiting for you. Your rolls are cut and boxed the moment your order lands.

Packed to travel

Chilled boxes, separated sauces, rice that's still soft when it reaches you. We tested a lot of containers.
